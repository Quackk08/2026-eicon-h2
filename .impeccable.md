## Design Context

### Users

ReNew is for people of all ages who want to turn a desired direction in life into realistic actions they can take today. The landing page serves first-time visitors who need to understand the product quickly, trust its non-medical approach, and decide whether to begin.

### Brand Personality

Cinematic, editorial, human.

### Aesthetic Direction

The landing page should feel like the opening of a quiet film followed by an independent print journal. It uses a full-bleed film hero, strong display typography, generous editorial spacing, and image-led storytelling. It must not resemble a generic SaaS landing page, a clinical mental-health service, or a decorative card grid.

### Design Principles

- Lead with a real-life mood and a clear ReNew wordmark before explaining features.
- Tell one continuous story from long-term direction to today's smallest realistic action.
- Use editorial composition, varied section rhythm, and restrained motion instead of repeated cards.
- Treat mobile browsers as a first-class experience with the complete content flow and touch-safe controls.
- Explain well-being, privacy, and support without diagnosis language, risk scores, or pressure.
- Present the e-ICON and SDG 3 connection after the ReNew product story is complete.
